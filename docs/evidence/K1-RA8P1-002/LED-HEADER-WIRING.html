Wire the LED stick to header U18

Titan Mini, hardware V1.0. Header part PH-00650. Sheet INTERFACE in Titan_Mini_schematic_v1.0.pdf, 26 March 2026. Pin order matches that sheet. This picture is not a millimetre drawing of the board.

Power off first. Move the second data wire to pin 12. Leave pin 16 and pin 33 empty. Break the link between LED 80 and LED 81.
1 Leave the first data wire on pin 7. That half is LEDs 1 to 80.
2 Put the second data wire on pin 12. That half is LEDs 81 to 160. Pin 33 stays empty.
3 Open the link between LED 80 and LED 81. Each half has its own data input.

Header face, counting from pin 1

Pin 1 is the +3.3 V pin at the marked end. Odd pins are the left column. Even pins are the right column. Find the pin-1 mark on the connector before counting. 5 V for the stick is pin 2 or pin 4. Ground for the stick is pin 6.

Pin 7 stays — lane A, P601 Pin 16 left open — old P004 wire Pin 12 takes that wire — lane B, P604 Pin 33 stays empty — P603 is not used Pins 2 and 4 — 5 V Pin 6 — ground

The 160-LED stick

One stick, two separate halves of 80. The level shifter already on the stick stays in both data paths. The two header pins are the shifter inputs.

Leave these alone

Pin 16 and pin 33Both empty. The second wire is only on pin 12.
Pins 3, 5, 8, 10 and 11Not this wire. Pins 3 and 5 are the USB data pair. Pins 8 and 10 have no GTIOC timer output. Pin 11 has no timer output.
Pin 13.3 V. It does not power the LEDs.
LED 80 to LED 81Data link open. The two inputs are two halves, not a spare copy of one signal.
Level shifter74HCT2G34GW stays on both data lines, with its own supply and the common ground.
E2 LiteDebugger only. It is not a wire to this header.

Firmware names the same LEDs from zero: pixels 0–79 are LEDs 1–80, pixels 80–159 are LEDs 81–160. The centre pair is LED 80 and LED 81. Do this with the supply off. The program on the board now sends the second half out of pin 12. Pin 33 stays empty.
